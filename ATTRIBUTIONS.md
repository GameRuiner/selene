# Asset attributions

## Surface textures

The Sun, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune textures are
from [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), based on
NASA imagery and elevation data. They are redistributed without pixel changes
under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Solar System Scope notes
that some maps use enhanced color or fictional fill where source coverage is
missing.

The following low-resolution mission mosaics are redistributed without pixel
changes from the U.S. Geological Survey Astrogeology Science Center. The model
fills unmapped black pixels with the body's neutral material color at render
time; the source image files remain unchanged.

- [Io Voyager/Galileo global mosaic](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/io_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_1km) — NASA/JPL/USGS, public domain.
- [Europa Voyager/Galileo global mosaic](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/europa_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_500m) — NASA/JPL/USGS, no use constraints listed.
- [Ganymede Voyager/Galileo global mosaic](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/ganymede_voyager_galileo_ssi_global_mosaic_1km) — NASA/JPL/USGS, public domain.
- [Callisto Galileo/Voyager global map](https://astrogeology.usgs.gov/search/map/callisto_galileo_voyager_simple_cylindrical_global_map) — NASA/JPL/USGS.
The existing Earth and Moon textures are bundled from the [Three.js planet
texture examples](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/tree/dev/examples/textures/planets):
[Earth](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/earth_atmos_2048.jpg) and
[Moon](https://threejs.org/examples/textures/planets/moon_1024.jpg). Three.js is
distributed under the [MIT license](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js/blob/dev/LICENSE).
